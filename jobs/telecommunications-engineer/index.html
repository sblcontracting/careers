Telecommunications Engineer

Saudi Arabia · Full-time · Posted 2026-11-05

SBL is looking for an experienced Telecom Engineer for our infrastructure and building projects across Saudi Arabia.

What you'll do

  • Design and supervise telecom networks: fiber optics (OSP/ISP), structured cabling and ELV systems
  • Supervise installation, splicing, OTDR testing and commissioning
  • Prepare and review drawings, BOQs and technical submittals
  • Coordinate with operators (STC, Mobily, Zain), consultants and subcontractors to CST standards

What we're looking for

  • Bachelor's degree in Telecommunications or Electronics Engineering
  • 7+ years of experience in Saudi Arabia
  • Valid Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE) membership
  • FTTx, structured cabling and ELV (CCTV, access control, fire alarm) experience is a plus
  • AutoCAD
  • Valid Saudi driving licence
  • Transferable Iqama (non-Saudis)

What we offer

  • Competitive salary based on experience
  • Housing and transportation (or allowances)
  • Medical insurance
  • Diverse projects and long-term stability
